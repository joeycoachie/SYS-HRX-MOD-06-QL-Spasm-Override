QL Spasm Override

Ipsilateral Hip Hike / Nerve Impingement

Session Clock

00:00

Medical Overrides

Week 1: Decompression & Glide

Passive Lengthening & Neural Flossing

Execution Sequence

  • Block A (Ladder Barrel): Passive Lateral Flexion. Right side facing ceiling. Deep side-bend over apex. Hold 90s. Breathe into right ribcage.
  • Block B (Wunda Chair): Static Pelvic Hold (Regression). Left foot on chair, right hovering. Hold 30s. Zero pelvic shift. NO STEPPING.
  • Block C (Reformer): Sciatic Glide. Single Leg Foot-in-Strap. Point toe at bottom of arc, flex hard toward shin at the top.

The Wolf Cue

Eccentrically lengthen the right Quadratus Lumborum before we ask the pelvis to bear weight. Restore glide to the sciatic tract without compressing the lumbar spine.

The Shepherd Cue

"Floss the nerve. Pull the silk thread smoothly through the back of your leg."

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Client reports shooting/electrical pain or tingling during the Sciatic Glide, indicating severe nerve entrapment rather than tension.

RA OVERRIDE: WEEK 1

Ensure the Ladder Barrel passive stretch does not hyperextend the bottom side ribs/spine. Support the head with a block to avoid cervical strain.

Week 2: Isometric Anchoring

Contralateral Load & Active Stability

Execution Sequence

  • Block A (Ladder Barrel): Active Lateral Flexion. Lower over barrel, actively use QL/Oblique to return to neutral. No hyperextension.
  • Block B (Wunda Chair): Lateral Step-Downs. Left foot anchored on chair, right hovering. 1 Heavy Spring. Slowly lower right foot by bending left knee, drive up.
  • Block C (Reformer): Sciatic Glide + Core Anchor. Add contralateral tabletop leg while flossing the working leg in the strap.

The Wolf Cue

The pelvis must remain perfectly level on the Chair. If the right hip hikes on the way up, the left Gluteus Medius is failing and the QL is taking over.

CRITICAL Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Visible right hip hiking during the upward phase of the Wunda Chair Step-Down. Immediate regression back to Week 1 isometric hold required.

RA OVERRIDE: WEEK 2

Substitute Wunda Chair Step-Downs with Supine Bridging (unilateral if tolerated) to eliminate standing knee shear.

Week 3: Dynamic Integration

Impact Recovery & Rotational Load

Execution Sequence

  • Block A (Ladder Barrel): Lateral Flexion to Rotation (Thread the Needle). Add transverse torque to the lengthened QL.
  • Block B (Wunda Chair): Lateral Step-Downs with offset Dumbbell. Contralateral load added to standing leg.
  • Block C (Reformer): Jumpboard (Light Spring). Reintroduce impact safely. Focus on mid-foot strike and rapid dorsiflexion recovery.

The Wolf Cue

The tendon/fascia has adapted. Now we train it to absorb shock and fire rapidly. On the Jumpboard, the ankle must decelerate the carriage, not the lower back.

Safety Gate (QA Fail)

Landing heavy on the heels during the Jumpboard sequence, sending percussive shockwaves straight up the sciatic tract into the lumbar spine.

RA OVERRIDE: WEEK 3

Jumping is strictly contraindicated. Remove Jumpboard. Perform rapid, light-spring footwork (continuous pumping) to achieve muscular taxation without joint impact.
